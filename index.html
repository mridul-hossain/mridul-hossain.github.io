Projects that show how I think

In development at Deets Software Ltd

AutoMarket AI

An Agent-as-a-Service platform that works as an autonomous "digital CMO" for small businesses and marketing agencies, planning campaigns, creating content and refining strategy.

The problem
Small teams can't staff a full marketing department, and unsupervised AI can't be trusted with budget or brand.
My approach
Five specialized agents share typed state in a LangGraph state machine. Ads and email run in parallel, and weak KPIs loop back to strategy.
Keeping it safe
RAG on pgvector keeps output on-brand. Anything that spends money or publishes goes to a person for Approve, Reject or Revise.
